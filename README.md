# dropeg

Carwash, Anywhere!WashDrop is Egypt’s carwash app that brings the car wash to you, making it effortless to keep your car shining and clean.
All You Car’s Needs Are Here.Whatever you need to service, and maintain your car is here. Served only by the best workshops and service-centers in Egypt. Ensuring your vehicle’s top-notch performance.

What the Drop application offers:
✨ Carwash, Anywhere:
A unique experience with WashDrop, the innovative car wash system that comes to you wherever you are, using eco-friendly SteamTech technology. Wash and shine anytime, anywhere!
🚘 Effortless Car Ownership:
Say goodbye to the hassle of car maintenance with Drop. Easily schedule maintenance appointments, track your car's service history, and even customize it according to your taste.
🔧 Reliable Repairs:
When your car needs repair, Drop is at your service. We collaborate with trusted workshops in Egypt to provide expert repair services.
🚀 Customization for the Cool:
For car enthusiasts, Drop offers customization services to transform your car into the coolest in the city.
And more fantastic features
